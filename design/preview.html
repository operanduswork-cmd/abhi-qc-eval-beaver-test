01 · Brand
QC Evaluator  ·  design system  ·  01 palette
Two colours.
That’s the whole system.
Palette and type ramp extracted from a live reference stylesheet rather than invented — hex values and an open-licence typeface, no marks or identity. Ink and accent are the same colour. There is nothing to spend sparingly, so emphasis comes from weight, scale and surface instead of hue — which is also why it cannot drift into the accent-on-neutral look that reads as machine-generated.
Ground & ink
Three surface values do all the structural work. No shadows anywhere in the system.

02 · Type
QC Evaluator  ·  design system  ·  02 type
Three faces
Poppins is display only, and never above weight 700. The reference uses it everywhere at 800; we don’t. Geometric faces gain presence from size and tight tracking, not from mass — 62px at 600 with −.045em reads sharper and more expensive than 48px at 800. Below 20px it hands over to the system stack entirely.
Ramp
Their scale, unchanged: 12 · 14 · 16 · 24 · 36 · 48 · 72. What changed is weight and tracking — every display size dropped a step in weight and tightened by ~.01em.

The rule that makes it a system
Not decoration. A reader learns it in ten seconds without being told, and it is a precise visual statement of the architecture.
MONOSPACE
Anything machine-measured — scores, line numbers, talk-share, cap ids, hashes, timestamps.
Code wrote this.
SANS
Anything judged — reasoning, the brief, quick fixes, red flags, quoted speech.
The model wrote this.
Test for any new element: could a person have disagreed with it? If yes it is sans. If it fell out of arithmetic or a string match, it is mono.
Micro-labels
Mono, 10px, +.12em, uppercase, ink at 38%. They name a region; they never carry content.

03 · Icons
QC Evaluator  ·  03 iconography
Fine line,
large form.
Weight comes from size, not from stroke. A 1.25px line on a 24px grid reads finer and more precise than the 1.5-on-20 default every icon library ships — and rendering it larger keeps the mass. Sleek and bold are not opposites here; they are different axes.
Why 1.25 / 24
Same glyph, three constructions. The default is heavier without being clearer.
