Cap notice — three states
A boolean cannot express coaching-01, where supporting and contradicting evidence both verify. The third state exists so the report can say “cannot verify” instead of guessing.
FIRED
No action steps stated for either party before close → max 70 total
INDETERMINATE
Next call booked live — cannot be verified. L188 agrees a time; L193 withdraws it. Scored the unverified branch: D10 → 0/5.
NOT FIRED
Coach talk-share 66.6% (63.1–70.4% at r=0.8–1.2) — below 75% on every reading.
the interval is shown because the cap is phrased in TIME and we can only measure words
