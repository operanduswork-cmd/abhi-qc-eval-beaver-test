Fine line,
large form.
Weight comes from size, not from stroke. A 1.25px line on a 24px grid reads finer and more precise than the 1.5-on-20 default every icon library ships — and rendering it larger keeps the mass. Sleek and bold are not opposites here; they are different axes.
Why 1.25 / 24
Same glyph, three constructions. The default is heavier without being clearer.
