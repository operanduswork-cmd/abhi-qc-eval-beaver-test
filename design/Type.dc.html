Three faces
Poppins is display only, and never above weight 700. The reference uses it everywhere at 800; we don’t. Geometric faces gain presence from size and tight tracking, not from mass — 62px at 600 with −.045em reads sharper and more expensive than 48px at 800. Below 20px it hands over to the system stack entirely.

Their scale, unchanged: 12 · 14 · 16 · 24 · 36 · 48 · 72. What changed is weight and tracking — every display size dropped a step in weight and tightened by ~.01em.
